ZenTomato Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

简体中文

ZenTomato ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles data and how you can contact us.

iOS Version

The initial iOS release is a paid download app. It does not include in-app purchases, advertising, account login, or cloud leaderboard features. Timer settings, focus sound preferences, statistics, achievement progress, and notification preferences are stored only on your device.

We do not collect User ID, purchase history, product interaction data, contact information, location, diagnostics, or advertising data from the iOS version, and we do not use any data for tracking.

Android Version

The Android version is planned for regions outside mainland China and may include a cloud leaderboard. When the cloud leaderboard is used, the App may store nickname, anonymous user ID, focus statistics, and leaderboard data through Supabase. This data is used only for leaderboard and App functionality, not for advertising tracking.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store: used for iOS paid download and payment processing.
  • Supabase: used only for the Android cloud leaderboard feature.

Data Deletion and Contact

If you would like to request deletion of Android leaderboard nickname or statistics data, or if you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

https://github.com/transbot/ZenTomato/issues

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's data handling practices change. Updated versions will be posted on this page.
