ZenTomato Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

简体中文

ZenTomato ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App may collect, how that data is used, and how you can contact us.

Data We Collect

To provide focus timer, statistics, leaderboard, and premium purchase features, the App may collect the following data:

  • User ID: used to identify anonymous users, sync statistics, restore purchases, and verify premium entitlements.
  • Purchase history: used to determine whether a user has purchased premium access, restore purchases, and unlock related features.
  • Product interaction data: such as focus duration, completed timer sessions, streaks, goal progress, and leaderboard data.
  • User content: such as the nickname a user sets for the leaderboard.

How We Use Data

The data above is used only for App functionality, including:

  • Providing focus statistics, goal progress, achievements, and leaderboard features.
  • Processing premium purchases, restoring purchases, and verifying entitlements.
  • Keeping services stable, syncing necessary data, and preventing entitlement loss.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services to provide necessary functionality:

  • RevenueCat: used to process App Store in-app purchases, purchase status, and premium entitlements.
  • Supabase: used to store leaderboard, nickname, and focus statistics data.
  • Apple App Store: used to process in-app purchases and payments.

Advertising and Tracking

The App currently does not show third-party ads. We do not use User ID, purchase history, or product interaction data for cross-app or cross-website tracking, targeted advertising, advertising measurement, or sharing with data brokers.

Local Data

Some settings and timer state may be stored only on your device, such as theme, timer settings, audio preferences, and notification preferences. Local storage alone does not transmit this data to us.

Data Deletion and Contact

If you would like to request deletion of leaderboard nickname or statistics data, or if you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

https://github.com/transbot/ZenTomato/issues

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's data handling practices change. Updated versions will be posted on this page.
